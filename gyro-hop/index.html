GyroHop

A push-to guide for manual telescopes, for iPhone and Android.

Clamp your phone to the tube, align on a star you know, and the app shows which way to push the telescope to land a galaxy, cluster or planet in the eyepiece. It works offline, and your location stays on your phone.
