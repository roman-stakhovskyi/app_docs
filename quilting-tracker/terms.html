The calculator

How much fabric the app works out is an estimate built on stated assumptions, and it states them on the screen: the usable width of the bolt, the seam allowance, ten per cent for cutting waste, and the layout your block size gives. Those assumptions differ by the units you work in — yards and inches, or metres and centimetres — because a metric shop sells a different bolt and cuts to a different smallest length. Fabric, shrinkage and the way you cut will differ again. Check the numbers against your own pattern before you buy or cut.
