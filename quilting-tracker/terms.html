The calculator

The yardage the app works out is an estimate built on stated assumptions: the usable width of the bolt, the seam allowance, ten per cent for cutting waste, and the layout your block size gives. Fabric, shrinkage and the way you cut will differ. Check the numbers against your own pattern before you buy or cut.
