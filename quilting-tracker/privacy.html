Privacy Policy

Quilting Tracker · last updated 15 September 2026

The short version. Your quilts, your fabric and your photos stay on your phone. There is no account to make and no server of ours holding your work. Three things can leave the phone, and each one only because you did something: a message you send us, a crash report, and a backup you switch on to your own iCloud or Google Drive.

What the app keeps, and where

Everything you type or photograph — projects, stages, notes, fabric, amounts, prices, shopping lists, settings and the photos themselves — is written to storage that belongs to the app on your own device. We cannot read it. It is removed when you delete the app.

Photos

The app asks for the camera or your photo library only when you add a picture, and only the pictures you pick are copied into the app's own folder. It never scans your library.

Backup, if you turn it on

Backup is off until you switch it on.

  • iPhone and iPad: a copy goes to the app's own container in your iCloud Drive, under your Apple Account. Apple holds it; we have no access to it.
  • Android: if you connect a Google account, a copy goes to the hidden app-data folder of your own Google Drive. The app asks for one permission, drive.appdata, which can only see the folder it created — never the rest of your Drive. You can disconnect at any time, and delete the folder from Google's own Drive settings.
  • Separately, Android's own Auto Backup may copy app data as part of the system backup of your phone. That is a Google feature, configured in your device settings.

When you write to us

If you send a message through Contact Support or post a feature request, we receive what you wrote, the email address you give us if you give one, the app's version, and a random install identifier that lets us tie a reply to the right conversation. That identifier is not your name, your phone number or your advertising id, and it is created by the app itself.

Crash reports

Released builds send a report when the app crashes: the error and where in the code it happened, the app version, the platform and OS version, the device model, and the same random install identifier. No quilt, fabric, note or photo is included.

Purchases

If you buy the one-off Pro unlock, the payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your card, your billing address or your account details — only whether this install owns the purchase.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of anything with anyone.
  • No location, contacts, microphone or health data.

Children

The app is made for quilters and is not directed at children under 13. It asks for a first name only so that the home screen can say hello, and that name never leaves the phone.

Your choices

  • Delete everything: remove the app from the phone.
  • Stop backing up: disconnect in Backup & sync, then delete the backup from your own Drive or iCloud.
  • Ask what we hold from a support message, or ask us to delete it: write to the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the updated page replaces this one.
