Privacy Policy

Quilting Tracker · last updated 7 November 2026

The short version. Your quilts, fabric and photos are kept on your phone, and — with Pro — copied to your own iCloud Drive or Google Drive so your other devices have them. There is no account to make, and no server of ours holds your work. Some things leave the phone without you doing anything: a crash or error report, and a check at launch whether this version still works. Others leave only when you do something: a message or Feature Request you send us, and a vote on the feature board. We keep messages so we can answer them, delete them whenever you ask, and may delete them sooner ourselves.

What the app keeps on your phone

Everything you type or photograph — projects, stages, notes, fabric, amounts, prices, shopping lists, your first name, your settings and the photos themselves — is written to storage that belongs to the app on your own device. We cannot read it. Deleting the app removes it from that phone; a copy in your own iCloud or Google Drive, or in a backup of the phone, stays until you delete it there.

Photos

The app asks for the camera or your photo library only when you add a picture, and only the pictures you pick are copied into the app's own folder. It never scans your library.

Sync between your devices (Pro)

With Pro, the app keeps a copy of your projects, fabric, shopping list, your first name, your units, currency and language, and your photos in your own cloud storage, and merges in what your other devices changed. Where two devices changed the same thing, the later change wins. Something you delete on one device is deleted on the others.

  • iPhone and iPad: the copy goes to the app's own folder in your iCloud Drive, under your Apple Account. Sync starts by itself once you have Pro and are signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive on. It stops when Pro ends, or when you sign out of iCloud or turn iCloud Drive off on that device.
  • Android: sync starts only when you connect a Google account in Backup & sync. The copy goes to a hidden folder in that account's Google Drive which only this app can open; the app asks Google for permission to that folder alone, never the rest of your Drive. The app learns the account's email address so it can show you which account it uses; that address stays on the phone and is never sent to us. Disconnecting withdraws the app's access; the copy in Drive stays where it is.

A photo you remove is deleted from your cloud once none of your devices uses it any more. A device that stops syncing — an old phone, or an app you uninstalled — leaves its last copy in your cloud, photos included, until you delete it there yourself (see “Your choices” below). When Pro ends, sync stops and the copy in your cloud is left as it is.

We have no access to any of this. It sits with Apple or Google under your own account.

Backups of your phone

  • Android: Android's own Auto Backup may copy the app's records — projects, fabric, shopping list, settings and your first name — as part of the system backup of your phone, but not the photos. Moving to a new phone with Google's transfer tool carries the photos too. That is a Google feature, configured in your device settings.
  • iPhone and iPad: your device's own iCloud or computer backup includes the app's data and photos, under your Apple Account.

When you write to us, or use the feature board

If you send a message through Contact Support or post a Feature Request, we receive what you wrote, the email address you give us if you give one, the app's version, whether you use iOS or Android, and a random install identifier that lets us tie a reply to the right conversation. That identifier is not your name, your phone number or your advertising id, and it is created by the app itself.

A Feature Request is public inside the app. Its title and description appear on the board for everyone who uses Quilting Tracker, so do not put anything personal in them. Your email address never appears there.

Opening the board and voting send the same install identifier, which is stored with your vote.

A support conversation is kept so we can answer it and so the answer is still there if you write again; a Feature Request is kept because the board is the list of what people have asked for. We do not use any of it for anything else. You are not required to write to us or to use the board, and nothing in the app depends on it.

Crash and error reports

Released versions send a report automatically when the app crashes or an operation fails unexpectedly — a sync that errors, for example. A report carries technical details of the error, the time, the app version, the platform and OS version, the device model, and the same random install identifier. A report is about the code, not about your content: we do not put your quilts, notes or photos in it. It carries no name or email, though the install identifier is the same one a message you send us carries.

The check at launch

Each time a released version starts with an internet connection, it checks with our service that this version is still supported. The request carries the app's identity and, as any internet request does, your network (IP) address — nothing you have entered.

Network addresses

Like any internet service, ours sees the network (IP) address each request comes from; we use it only to stop abuse and keep it no longer than that needs.

Purchases

If you buy Pro, yearly or for good, the payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your card, your billing address or your account details — only whether this install owns the purchase, so the app can give you what you paid for.

Why we hold it

In the language of the GDPR, each use above rests on one of these grounds:

  • Performing our agreement with you: giving you Pro once you have bought it, and syncing your data to your own cloud when Pro includes it.
  • Our legitimate interest in answering you: support messages, Feature Requests and votes — answering the person who wrote, knowing what people have asked for, and being able to show what was said if a dispute about the app ever arises.
  • Our legitimate interest in an app that works: crash and error reports, which tell us what is broken, and the check at launch, which lets us stop a version that would damage your data or no longer works.
  • Our legitimate interest in a service that stays up: using network addresses to stop abuse.

Where we rely on a legitimate interest, you may object, and we will stop unless our reason outweighs yours. We make no automated decisions about you.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No selling of anything to anyone, and no sharing for anyone else's purposes.
  • No location, contacts, microphone or health data.

Who else sees it

  • Our support and crash-reporting service — support messages, Feature Requests, votes, crash and error reports, the check at launch and network addresses go there, and nowhere else of ours. It runs on a cloud hosting provider in the United States, which processes the data for us and for no purpose of its own. Transfers outside the European Economic Area rely on the European Commission's adequacy decision for the United States (the EU–US Data Privacy Framework) or on the Commission's standard contractual clauses.
  • Apple — the App Store sale, iCloud Drive if you sync from an iPhone or iPad, and your device backup. Apple holds those under your Apple Account, not ours (Apple's privacy policy).
  • Google — the Google Play sale, signing in to Google and Google Drive if you connect an account on Android, and Android's Auto Backup. Google holds those under your Google Account (Google's privacy policy).
  • Other people using the app — the title and description of a Feature Request you post, and nothing else.
  • The web host of these pages — it logs the network address of whoever opens them, for security. The pages set no cookies and load nothing from anyone else; even the typeface is served from the same place.

How long things are kept

  • On your phone: until you delete it, or delete the app.
  • In your own iCloud or Google Drive (Pro): until you delete it there. A removed photo goes once none of your devices uses it; a device that stopped syncing keeps its copy there until you delete it; when Pro ends, the copy stays as it is.
  • Backups of your phone: for as long as Apple or Google keep them under your account.
  • Messages and Feature Requests: until you ask us to remove them — see below — or until we remove them ourselves, which we may do at any time. We do not delete them on a timer, because a conversation you come back to is more use to you whole.
  • Votes: for as long as the feature board exists.
  • Crash and error reports: 90 days, then each report is deleted automatically.
  • Network addresses: only as long as stopping abuse needs.

Your choices

  • Stop syncing: on Android, disconnect in Backup & sync. On an iPhone or iPad, sync stops when Pro ends, or when you sign out of iCloud or turn iCloud Drive off in Settings.
  • Delete the copy in your cloud: on an iPhone or iPad, in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage, choose Quilting Tracker and delete its data. On Android, in Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps, choose Quilting Tracker and delete its hidden app data. It is in your account, so it is yours to remove.
  • Delete everything on the phone: remove the app. That does not remove a copy in your cloud or in a backup of your phone; delete those where they are.

Deleting a message or a Feature Request

Write to the address on this page and say what you want removed. Ask from the email address you used when you wrote to us: that address is how we find your messages, and we have no other way to tell which are yours.

We delete them from our service, along with anything else attached to that address, within 30 days, and reply when it is done.

If you wrote without giving an email address, there is no address to match, and nothing in what we hold points back to you. That also means we cannot find it to delete it. The same is true of votes and crash reports: they carry only the install identifier, which you cannot see, so we cannot pick out yours.

Your rights

Wherever you live, and in the EU and the UK as a matter of law, you may ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict what we do with it, or hand it to you in a form you can take elsewhere; and you may object to us holding it at all. Ask at the address on this page.

If you think we have handled your data badly and our answer does not satisfy you, you may complain to the data protection authority of the country you live in. We would rather you wrote to us first, but that right is yours either way.

Children

The app is made for quilters and is not directed at children under 13. It asks for a first name only so that the home screen can say hello. That name is never sent to us; with Pro it is copied to your own cloud with the rest of your data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the updated page replaces this one. If a change affects what leaves your phone, the store notes of the update that brings it say so, so you know before you install it.
